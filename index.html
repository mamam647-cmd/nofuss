your event, sorted.

From one dinner to thirty events a year. Pip forecasts who'll actually show up, sends the invites and builds the orders. Nothing goes out, and no money moves, without your OK.

how it works

  1. 1 bring your event, or start fresh Connect Luma and Pip picks up your guest list and replies. Starting fresh? Six quick questions.
  2. 2 get a plan built on who'll come Pip forecasts from real RSVPs, sizes food and drinks to match, and re-plans when things change.
  3. 3 approve, and you're done Every message, change and order waits for your OK. Money only moves when you tap.
